# Radiant Star Girdle (a girdle with a skirt of silk panels, on the hips)

`Radiant_Star_Girdle.vd`: all 35 actions x 5 directions (1050 frames), layer `skirt`, made from one Meshy AI model (`Meshy_AI_Radiant_Star_Girdle_1022181159_texture.glb`, 47 MB, 265k vertices, textures 4096 px; **not in the repo**: keep it as `models/radiant_star_girdle.glb`, the folder is in `.gitignore`; record the licence when it is known).

Rebuild (about 10 min for the item and the cloth, ~15 min for the render):
`python pipeline/uo_make_item.py model/items/radiant_star_girdle/girdle.json --out OUT --vd`.
Rendering in several processes: give every process its own output folder (they share `_tmp.png` otherwise), then merge the `frames/` folders and run once more with `ONLY=["04_stand"]` to write the combined `.vd`.

What was decided (measured, see the commit messages and README section 3a):
- **On the hips, not too big.** The waistband (0.42 x 0.33 m at the model's scale) sits at z 1.0-1.1 m (`NUDGE` -0.25 m), `stretch` 0.87 x 0.85 narrows it to the hips (0.35 x 0.28 m) and `"prepare": {"FIT": false}` keeps the push-out fit from inflating the ring to 0.67 x 0.61 m. The hem ends above the ankles (`HEM` 0.12 m; the ankle joint is at 0.10 m): 9-17 cm above the ground standing and walking, it touches the ground only when the body crouches (attacks, falls).
- **Silk that is attracted to nothing.** `"sim": {"engine": "pbd", "damp": 3}` = `uo_cloth_pbd.py`: only the waistband follows the skeleton, the rest hangs free (gravity, stretch and bending constraints, air drag); legs and pelvis are capsules the cloth is pushed out of in every sub-step, so the legs go through the slits and the panels fall back by their own weight. Blender's cloth crumpled this skirt into ropes in the walk in every variant (quality, margins, mass x1-x3, pin 0-0.15). `damp` 3 is the calmer ("heavier") setting; 1.5 swings more.
- **Texture.** 4096 px baked from the original (no smearing by the 16k-vertex reduction), used as it is (`"materials": {"TEXTURE_FILTER": false}`), and every pixel is the area average of a 4 x 4 grid of sub-pixel camera positions (scene `uo_subpixel` 4, 1024 samples in all). See "Texture: area average" at the end; `Radiant_Star_Girdle_low_v4.vd` is the current file.
- **No dark inside.** Scene `uo_behind_torso`: the torso hides the inside of the waistband ring (it showed as a dark brown area over the chest).
- **Smooth edges.** Scene `uo_edge_smooth` 3 (renderer: 1-px spikes of the outline dropped, 1-px notches filled): 8.3 -> 0.35 spikes and 4.4 -> 0.6 notches per frame, the area changes by 1 % (stand, walk, run, slash; 140 frames).
- **Stretch.** `uo_cloth_pbd.py` `iters` 60 (default): with 10 the hanging skirt stretched by 0.15 m under its own weight and lay on the ground, with 60 by 2 cm.
- Mounted actions (23-29): the hem follows the legs (not simulated), as for every skirt.

QA (`item_qa.py`, item inside the body in the posed frames): mean 14.1 % (worst 21_die_forward), deepest 127 mm. Higher than a fitted item (6.8 % with the fit) because the band is not pushed out of the skin; the renderer pushes it out of limbs, and the torso never hides items.

## Second version: lower on the hips (`girdle_low.json` -> `Radiant_Star_Girdle_low.vd`)

The band sits 7 cm lower (`NUDGE` -0.32 m: band at z 0.98-1.08 m, on the line of underwear), the hem stays at the same height above the ankles (`HEM` 0.13 m), so the skirt is about 5 cm shorter. ### Filling the gap between the banner and its outer gold frame (`pipeline/uo_fill_banner_gap.py`)

The model has a central banner with a gold border and, 4 cm outside and 4 cm behind it, a thin outer frame (two vertical strips and a V); between them there are no faces, so the skirt's back or the body shows through. `python pipeline/uo_fill_banner_gap.py models/radiant_star_girdle.glb models/radiant_star_girdle_filled.glb --png mask.png` adds 28.5k triangles (0.128 m2, two pieces of 0.052 m2) to the glb itself (images and the rest byte for byte): the gap is found in the front view (the ring of the front-layer faces, holes filled), the depth is interpolated from the neighbours (+2 mm behind them), and every new triangle takes the colour chart of a plain-cloth triangle next to the border (the atlas is a mosaic of small charts, no map (x, z) -> uv exists). Not yet used by `girdle.json` (it still points at the original glb).

### Narrower side panels (`pipeline/uo_narrow_panels.py`)

In the game the side panels are 4-6 px wide each (measured on the stand frames, 1 px = 2.8 cm), so "4 px thinner" was taken as 4 px in all: every vertex outside |x| = 0.26 of the model moves towards the middle by the same factor k = 0.77 (|x'| = 0.26 + (|x| - 0.26) k; the waistband and the banner are not touched, the factor fades to 1 over z 0.70..0.76): the outer edge of each panel goes in by 0.10 model units = 2.0 px at the hem (the hem tips' span 1.41 -> 1.20 units, 28 -> 24 px). `python pipeline/uo_narrow_panels.py models/radiant_star_girdle_filled.glb models/radiant_star_girdle_filled_narrow.glb --k 0.77`. Not yet used by the recipes.

### Lower version with the filled gap and the narrower panels: stand + walk only (`Radiant_Star_Girdle_low_stand_walk.vd`)

`girdle_low.json` now points at `models/radiant_star_girdle_filled_narrow.glb` (gap filled by `uo_fill_banner_gap.py`, panels narrowed by `uo_narrow_panels.py`), `sim` gets `"glue": 0.025`. The `.vd` has only `04_stand` and `00_walk_unarmed` (60 frames); the other actions are rendered the same way (`--vd`). Autofit scale 0.639 (0.631 before: the narrower mesh). Correction about the texture (found when packing the Blender file): the 4096 px texture baked from the original is box-averaged by `uo_materials.py` (`TEXTURE_FILTER`, `FILTER_TEXELS_PER_M` 54 = 1.5 texels per sprite pixel) down to 240 x 240 px in `item.blend`, before the render, in v1-v3. Superseded by the next section.

Texture: area average (owner asked to check a sharper texture, chose the "averaged" reference). Reference = the mean of each pixel (4 x 4 camera positions moved by sub-pixel steps, 64 samples each), measured on the stand frames (interior of the item, RMSE in levels of 255; the 1 px Cycles `filter_width` has no effect in this pipeline, so it cannot serve as the reference): v1-v3 (texture box-averaged to 54 texels/m = 240 px, one sample per pixel) 16.6; 108 texels/m (512 px) 8.9; filter off (full 4096, one sample per pixel) 6.9. The pre-averaging made things worse: the atlas is a mosaic of tiny charts, the box average mixes the colours of neighbouring charts (and the item came out 9 levels darker than the reference). Now `TEXTURE_FILTER` is off and `render_uo_layer.py` has the per-item option `uo_subpixel` (G: G x G renders with the camera shifted by sub-pixel steps, premultiplied mean; a single render at G times the resolution was not used: the normal-map derivatives follow the pixel footprint and it came out 3 levels darker). Result against the reference: 3.6 (noise level of the reference) instead of 6.6-9; the silhouette (alpha) differs from v3 by 1.5 px per frame on average (max 9), the colours by 16 levels RMSE (the texture is richer, a little warmer). Cost: 1050 frames in 4 processes about 1 h 15 min (`render.use_persistent_data` doubles the speed, identical pixels). Paperdoll gumps and the icon already averaged 3 x 3; re-rendered from the new texture, the silhouettes are identical (0 px), `paperdoll_low/`. `Radiant_Star_Girdle_low_v4.vd`.

## v5 (2026-10-29): the owner's list of defects, checked per frame

Source: the owner's notes on `Radiant_Star_Girdle_low_v4.vd` in the viewer. Current file: `Radiant_Star_Girdle_low_v5.vd` (1050 frames, recipe `girdle_low.json`, sim 210 frames, render 1024 samples, `uo_subpixel` 4).

1. The band does not hug the waist / hips (walk dir 2 f3, walk armed dir 2 f6, also from behind). Measured: the skinned band stood 2-4 cm off the posed skin in every frame (gap median 18 mm, p90 42 mm). `uo_cloth_pbd.py wrap=1` shrink-wraps the pinned band and the fade under it onto the skin of the posed body (target 6 mm): gap median 7 mm, p90 max 24 mm over 210 frames (walk p90 11 mm). The dark inside of the band that the hands showed is gone with it.
2. Panels merge into one patch (run armed dir 4 f3, walk armed dir 0 f1, bow dir 0 f2). New renderer option `DEPTH_LINE` (scene `uo_depth_line` 0.45, `uo_depth_thr` 0.035): a depth render per frame, a 1-px seam on the pixel that lies more than 3.5 cm behind a neighbouring item pixel. Tried 0.7 / 0.9 (black patches), 0.35 / 0.05 (hardly visible).
3. The hand cuts too much (attack 2H bash dir 0 f5). Measured with `cut_stats.json`: the share of the item that the body hides did not change (14 -> 15 % mean over the action); what looks cut is the thigh through the slit (the free render has the slit) and the forearm in front of the belly, both in front of the cloth in 3D. Changed: the arms and hands now collide with the cloth (signed distance field of the real body mesh, `sdf=1`, `project=0.008`: the capsules only had the legs), `uo_hide_erode` 1. The visible change is the band that now covers the belly and shows no dark inside.
4. Walk armed dir 0 f1: the side merges with the middle next to the leg: same fix as 2 and 1.
5. Riding (all mounted actions): the cloth was stretched along the legs (the "lie" of the renderer) and the side panel was wide. Now simulated (`mounted=1 horse=1`, scene `uo_cloth_mounted` 0): it drapes over the thighs and the flank of the horse, 3D checked in `view_pose` (body + horse + cloth); the strands of a few pixels below are removed (`uo_min_piece_mounted` 30). In the front views the horse neck is in front of the lap, so little cloth is visible there (area 8-45 % of the median of the action): correct for the horse mesh, not changed.
6. Bow dir 0 f2: middle strip joins the side: 2.

Check of every frame: sim and render of all 1050 frames, then `qc_frames.py`-style checks (pieces, area against the action, band gap, hidden share) and a look at tight crops of every action (rows = directions, columns = frames). Remaining flags (looked at in tight crops, left as they are): mounted front views (cloth behind the horse neck), the falls 21 / 22 (the body lies on the cloth, as in v4), 7 frames where a raised knee is in front of the cloth and the body hides 46-47 % of it (bash 1h dir 0 f4 / f5, pierce 2h dir 0 f4, spell dir 0 f4, dir 1 f3 / f4, bow dir 1 f3: the thigh passes the pinned band, which does not collide), 3 frames with 2 small pieces (run dir 1 f3, slash 2h dir 0 f1, fall dir 1 f1).

Tools of this round (not in the repo): body silhouette raster to compare the 3D body with the sprites, `view_pose` (3D view of body + horse + cloth of one frame), per-frame contact sheets.
