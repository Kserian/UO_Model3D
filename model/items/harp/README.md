# Harp (Celtic hand harp, strings removed) for the right hand, both hands when standing

`Harp_right_hand.vd`: all 35 actions x 5 directions (1050 frames).

Model: the user's glTF of a carved Celtic harp (Google Drive link given in the session, 44 MB, 300k vertices, 4096 px textures, **not in the repo**; **author and licence unknown: record them**). `pipeline/harp_prepare_model.py IN.glb harp.glb` makes `harp.glb` (2.2 MB, the file used here): the 20 string pieces are cut out (pale grey texture, longer than 0.4 m; the pale slivers left at the pins too), the thin arm of the V is turned upright on x = y = 0 (the side the harp is held by), 60k faces, 1024 px JPEG textures. The tuning pins' holes stay (not visible at 36 px/m). Matte (`METAL: false`), the colours of the model's own wood texture.

Rebuild: `python pipeline/uo_make_item.py model/items/harp/harp.json --out OUT --vd`.

- Size: 0.68 m high, 0.55 m wide (`HEIGHT` 0.68, `WIDEN` 1.25: the model is stretched along X so that the harp reaches the left hand, 0.5-0.6 m from the right one in the standing poses, without being taller than the torso); the shell is pushed out by `THICKEN` 1.9 cm (a 2 cm frame is under 1 px at 36 px/m).
- Held by the thin arm (the pillar) at half of its height in the right fist. Per action (all settings in `uo_harp.py`, the recipe's `"harp"` overrides them):
  - one hand (default): the pillar upright at the back, the rest of the harp (the sound box) forward; orientation = the chest's (no sword-like swinging); walking and running (`STEADY`: 0-3, 23, 24) = ONE orientation for the whole cycle, only the grip follows the hand.
  - walk, walk armed (`LEAN_WALK` 0, 1): held normally by the pillar in the right fist (the usual hold), tipped 20 deg towards the ground, held 3 cm further out (`WALK_OUT`) and leaned -4 deg (`WALK_LEAN`; + = the top out): the fist is at the middle of the pillar, so leaning the top out swung the lower half into the thigh (4-8 of 750 sample points inside at +12 deg, 0 at -4 deg), one constant orientation per action. The delivered vd has this pose in directions 0 and 4 only (user: the collision is seen there); directions 1-3 are the earlier +12 deg version.
  - area spell, mounted 1H attack (`YAW_OUT` 17, 26): the usual hold (pillar upright, the rest forward), turned 12 deg about the axis of the held side (the pillar) so the sound box leans outward, towards the arm. Area spell also follows the hand a little (`FOLLOW` 17): 20 % of the hand's rotation relative to the chest, smoothed over 5 frames, capped at 20 deg: it turns at most 2 deg per frame more than the chest alone and 29 deg in total, it never spins.
  - the two falls (the names of the .vd are misleading: **action 21 `die_forward` ends ON THE BACK, action 22 `die_backward` ends face down**):
    - 21 (`FLAT_OUT`; user: the harp landed under the body): the pillar along the arm's outward direction (shoulder -> hand) through the fist, held near its inner end (8 % of its height), so the whole harp lies on the floor beyond the hand, flat, the sound box to the feet's side, 57-74 cm from the chest; while the body is still upright it is the walk's hold, blended in by the tilt of the chest (20-70 deg).
    - 22 (`FLAT_DIE`; user: back to the earlier version): held in the hand, lying flat (plane horizontal), the sound box pointing away from the body, the pillar along the fist's horizontal axis (50-98 cm from the chest).
  - both hands (`TWO_HAND` 4, 5, 6, 25: stand, fidget 1+2, mounted stand): the pivot of the left fist 0-5 cm from the wood (outside it), the triangle faces the front (`FRONT_PLANE`). Stand (user: closer to the belly, parallel to it, like the fidget but static): 22 cm ahead of the chest (`AHEAD_MIN`/`AHEAD_MAX` 22-28), the plane within 15 deg of the belly's (`FACING_MAX`), the pillar 7 deg from vertical, 2.5 cm clear of the body (1 of 1000 sample points inside); fidget 1 is 25-27 cm ahead. Fidget 2: higher (`RAISE` 10-20 cm above the right fist), 25-35 cm ahead, may lean, clear of the body. Mounted stand (`PARALLEL` 25): parallel to the belly, pillar upright, no collisions. Per frame, smooth in time. The arms are the original animation's, only the harp moves.
  - salute, eat (`LEFT_HOLD` 33, 34; user): the harp in front of the body (27-35 cm ahead of the chest, plane facing the front), held by the LEFT fist by the OTHER side, the long arm of the sound box (the fist's axis within 0-37 deg of the arm's), the pillar hangs free; the right hand does its gesture, no collisions.
- Hiding by the body (user: no harp pixels on the body where the body is in front, in every direction): the harp carries `uo_behind_torso` (the torso hides it where it is behind the torso; by default the torso never hides an item) and the recipe sets `"scene": {"uo_torso_margin": 0.02, "uo_holdout_margin": 0.003}` (the head, arms, hands and legs hide it where they are more than 3 mm in front; the default is 1 cm). Both are per-item scene properties of `render_uo_layer.py`, the defaults are unchanged for every other item. Measured with `pipeline/item_hide_check.py` against an exact holdout of the whole body, all 35 actions x 5 directions: **0.12 % of the harp's pixels** are drawn where the body hides them (at most 9 px in a frame; before: 9.3 %, 26 % in stand / fidget 2 seen from behind), and at most 8 px per frame are hidden that the exact holdout shows.
- QA (`item_qa.py`): inside the body ~2.7 %, deepest 95 mm.

## Version with strings

`Harp_right_hand_strings.vd` (35 actions x 5 directions): the same harp and the same poses with 3 dark grey strings (`harp_strings.json`: `STRINGS` 3, `STRING_DIAM` 0.03, albedo 0.09), parallel to the pillar, evenly spaced between the pillar and the sound box arm (x 0.15 / 0.24 / 0.34 m from the pillar, 0.31 / 0.23 / 0.10 m long: the longest by the pillar). The strings are tubes built by `uo_harp.py` in the harp's frame after the scaling and `THICKEN`, with a material of their own. Their top end is the underside of the neck found by a ray cast from above (the first version read it from the vertices of the reduced mesh: the strings near the pillar started 12-16 cm below the neck; `Harp_right_hand_strings.vd` was rendered again with the fix: the 3 strings are 0.47 / 0.28 / 0.15 m long instead of 0.31 / 0.23 / 0.10). A real string (1 mm) or 14 mm is not drawn at 36 px/m, 3 cm is a 1-px line. A 1-px string is all rim: with the usual darkened edge it comes out nearly black (28), `uo_edge_floor` 54 makes it dark grey, BUT the floor lifts the dark rim of the whole wood to grey too (median RGB 55/54/54 instead of 60/41/20: "a grey border", user). So `Harp_right_hand_strings.vd` is composed (`pipeline/vd_compose_strings.py WOOD_RENDER STRINGS_RENDER OUT.vd`): the wood is exactly the render of the item WITHOUT strings (its brown rim), and only the pixels that exist in the strings render alone (the strings floating in the harp, 12 022 px in all frames, value 46-80, median 54) are added. All directions of every action have the final poses (the walk lean -4 deg also in directions 1-3).

## Replacing frames in a finished vd

`pipeline/vd_merge.py BASE.vd OUT.vd RENDER_DIR ACTION:DIRS ...` replaces single (action, direction) blocks with frames rendered later (`run_render_headless.py ... ONLY=[...]`), every other block stays byte for byte (a re-encoded unchanged block is identical to the original: checked). The delivered `Harp_right_hand.vd` was corrected this way: walk and walk armed directions 0 and 4, stand, both falls (19 blocks).

## Paperdoll: gumps and art (`paperdoll/no_strings`, `paperdoll/strings`)

`pipeline/uo_render_harp_gump.py GUMP.blend ART.blend OUT` (same 3D model, light and colours as the animation; the wood's colour matches the animation's: median RGB 104/64/32 against 107/71/38). Per version (no strings / strings):
- `gump_<hold>_<male|female>.png` + `.bin` (a Gumpart.mul entry; male 50000 + AnimID on body gump 12, female 60000 + AnimID on 13: the IDs are yours to choose), 260 x 237, front view, 90.5 px/m, the harp held by the pillar in the fist of the body gump (the pillar is cut out under the fist, the fist holds it):
  - `weapon`: like a weapon at the side of the right hand, the pillar leaning 15 deg out, the sound box out of the body (turned 45 deg to the viewer so it stays in the picture);
  - `play`: close to the body, as if about to play: the sound box over the torso, the pillar leaning 8 deg to the body, the hand lower on the pillar so the harp rises to the chest;
  - `weapon_left`, `play_left`: the same two holds in the OTHER hand (the character's left, the right of the picture), mirrored.
- `art.png` + `.bin` and `art_mirror.png` + `.bin` (art.mul, two IDs): the NEW item icon, the harp standing, seen like in the game (the camera's elevation 28.45 deg), turned 30 deg (3/4 view), leaning 10 deg back, 58 px/m: 32 x 41 px, a dark rim; the strings version has 5 strings drawn as 1-px lines (`harp_art_strings.json`: the animation's frame, 5 strings; the no-strings art is made from `harp.json`'s item). 46 px/m (24 x 33 px) was too small: 5 strings merged into one dark wedge.
- `art_lying.png` + `.bin`, `art_lying_mirror.png` + `.bin`: the earlier icon, the harp lying flat seen from above with the pillar along the diagonal, 44 x 28 px.
- `sheet.png`: the gumps over the body gumps (rows male / female, columns weapon, play, weapon_left, play_left).
The gump uses a thinner frame than the animation (`harp_gump.json`, THICKEN 0.004: 3-4 px at 90.5 px/m; the animation's 0.019 is 2-3 px at 36 px/m), the art the animation's item. Strings in the gumps: 7 dark grey ones (`harp_gump_strings.json`), drawn by `uo_render_harp_gump.py` as 1-px lines (grey 60) between their ends, not rendered as tubes: a 1.3-px tube broke into pieces (a pixel needs 50 % coverage; 20 % made solid stripes). They go under the wood and under the hand (the fist window), so every string runs from the neck to the arm. In the art 3 strings, the same. A string shorter than `STRING_MIN` 5 cm is not made (the span shrinks). The .bin files decode back to their PNGs (checked, 16 gumps).
