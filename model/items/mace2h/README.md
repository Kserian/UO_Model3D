# Two-handed spiked mace (blunt weapon)

`Mace2H.vd`: all 35 actions x 5 directions (1050 frames), made from one glTF model (`Meshy_AI_TwoHandedSpikedMace`, 12 MB, **not in the repo**: kept in `models/mace2h.glb` outside the repo when re-running; AI-generated model supplied by the user, record the licence when it is known).

Rebuild: `python pipeline/uo_make_item.py model/items/mace2h/mace2h.json --out OUT --vd` (about 15 min on 4 cores at the default 512 samples; `--preview` renders a contact sheet only).

What the recipe does (every setting is described in the header of `pipeline/uo_make_item.py`, `uo_spikes.py` and `uo_twohand.py`):
- **Size and spikes:** 1.35 m long (UO maces / hammers 1.06-1.29 m, 2H axes up to 1.65 m); the 9 spikes of the ball 1.6 x longer and 3 x thicker, then every spike 1 px (0.0278 m) shorter and thinner by the same ratio (`TRIM`), the shaft and pommel 1 px (0.0278 m) thicker in diameter (`uo_spikes.py`), so that they read at 36 px/m.
- **Grip:** the upper fist 5 px (0.14 m) under the bottom of the ball; mounted (23-29) 0.12 m lower.
- **Motion from the hands (`uo_twohand.py`)**, not from the halberd calibration of `polearm.L`: the shaft goes through both fists in every frame in which the fists can hold it together (2H attacks 12-14, idles 8 / 15, walk, run, block ...: the lower fist is 0.0 cm off the shaft); the upper hand is the right one in 8, 12, 13, 15 (as on the original 2H weapons), the left one in the rest. The ball does not spin about the shaft (the turn follows the chest). Where the fists cannot hold it together (spells, bows, 1H attacks, the mounted swing 29: fists closer than 18 cm or farther than the weapon is long, in most frames of the action) the weapon keeps the direction of the calibrated halberd line of that frame for the whole action (steady, no switching between two lines), the upper (left) fist at the 5 px grip.
- **Walk and run armed (1, 3):** the grip another 5 px (0.14 m) lower (`GRIP_EXTRA`), and the whole weapon moved forward along the chest by the least amount (0-150 mm in walk, 0-80 mm in run, smooth over the cycle) at which the shaft is 1.5 cm outside the posed skin (`FRONT_ACTIONS`); the fists then hold it from behind.
- **Combat idles and attacks (7-15) and the bow / crossbow attacks (18, 19):** the grip another 3.5 px (0.126 m) lower (`GRIP_EXTRA`); the lower fist stays at least 9 cm from the butt (`BUTT_MIN`, `BUTT_ACTIONS`) so that the end of the weapon still sticks out of the hand (9-46 cm beyond the fist in the frames where both fists hold it).
- **Salute and eat (33, 34):** the weapon is held by the left hand alone, upright (the direction of the calibrated halberd line), the fist at 3/4 of the shaft height (`GRIP_FRAC`, `SINGLE_ACTIONS`).
- **Falls (21, 22):** the fist at 3/4 of the shaft height; the shaft turns into the axis of the body (pelvis -> head, the ball towards the head) as the body tips over and lies along it (last frame 8 / 2 deg from the body axis); lifted when it would dig into the floor.
- **Riding (23-29):** the line of the fists is moved to the nearest direction in which the shaft (8 cm), the ball and the spike tips (7 cm) clear the horse proxy of the frame and the ball points away from the horse (side of the upper hand); intrusion 0 mm in 23-28, 27 mm in the swing 29.
- **Cuts:** the hands hide the shaft where they are in front of it; from directions 3 and 4 the back, neck and head hide it (`behind_torso`, `uo_torso_margin` 0.04); the original body outline cuts it to the pixel (`EXACT_BODY`).
- **Look:** metal and leather colours of the texture (`BRIGHTNESS` 2.1); outline 0.7 of the colour next to the rim, mixed 60% from the inside (`uo_outline_blend`): a darker shade of the surrounding colours, not black.
- QA: `python pipeline/test_twohand.py` (synthetic mace, 8 s).
