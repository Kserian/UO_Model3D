# Two-handed spiked mace (blunt weapon)

`Mace2H.vd`: all 35 actions x 5 directions (1050 frames), made from one glTF model (`Meshy_AI_TwoHandedSpikedMace`, 12 MB, **not in the repo**: kept in `models/mace2h.glb` outside the repo when re-running; AI-generated model supplied by the user, record the licence when it is known).

Rebuild: `python pipeline/uo_make_item.py model/items/mace2h/mace2h.json --out OUT --vd` (about 15 min on 4 cores at the default 512 samples; `--preview` renders a contact sheet only).

What the recipe does (every setting is described in the header of `pipeline/uo_make_item.py`, `uo_spikes.py` and `uo_twohand.py`):
- **Size and spikes:** 1.35 m long (UO maces / hammers 1.06-1.29 m, 2H axes up to 1.65 m); the 9 spikes of the ball 1.6 x longer and 3 x thicker, then every spike 2 px (0.0556 m) shorter and thinner by the same ratio (`TRIM`), the shaft and pommel 1 px (0.0278 m) thicker in diameter (`uo_spikes.py`), so that they read at 36 px/m.
- **Grip:** the upper fist 5 px (0.14 m) under the bottom of the ball; mounted (23-29) 0.12 m lower.
- **Motion from the hands (`uo_twohand.py`)**, not from the halberd calibration of `polearm.L`: the shaft goes through both fists in every frame in which the fists can hold it together (2H attacks 12-14, idles 8 / 15, walk, run, block ...: the lower fist is 0.0 cm off the shaft); the upper hand is the right one in 8, 12, 13, 15 (as on the original 2H weapons), the left one in the rest. The ball does not spin about the shaft (the turn follows the chest). Where the fists cannot hold it together (spells, bows, 1H attacks, the mounted swing 29: fists closer than 18 cm or farther than the weapon is long, in most frames of the action) the weapon keeps the direction of the calibrated halberd line of that frame for the whole action (steady, no switching between two lines), the upper (left) fist at the 5 px grip.
- **Walk and run armed (1, 3):** the grip another 5 px (0.14 m) lower (`GRIP_EXTRA`), and the whole weapon moved forward along the chest by the least amount (0-150 mm in walk, 0-80 mm in run, smooth over the cycle) at which the shaft is 1.5 cm outside the posed skin (`FRONT_ACTIONS`); the fists then hold it from behind.
- **Combat idles and attacks (7-15) and the bow / crossbow attacks (18, 19):** the grip another 3.5 px (0.126 m) lower (`GRIP_EXTRA`); the lower fist stays at least 9 cm from the butt (`BUTT_MIN`, `BUTT_ACTIONS`) so that the end of the weapon still sticks out of the hand (9-46 cm beyond the fist in the frames where both fists hold it).
- **Salute and eat (33, 34):** the weapon is held by the left hand alone, upright (the direction of the calibrated halberd line), the fist at 3/4 of the shaft height (`GRIP_FRAC`, `SINGLE_ACTIONS`).
- **Riding (23-28), one hand:** the weapon is held by the left hand alone, the shaft through the CENTRE of our 3D fist (`FISTC_ACTIONS`; checked against the original sprites: the fist lands on the hand the sprite shows), so it FOLLOWS the hand in every frame, fist at 3/4 of the shaft height (`GRIP_FRAC`). Direction: upright, mirrored outwards (butt end away from the body and the horse, `STAFF_SIDE` -1), ONE lean for the whole action (21-42 deg from vertical, `STAFF_ACTIONS`, `STAFF_MAX` 0.8), so it does not jump from frame to frame. Tried and rejected: the origin of the calibrated halberd as the grip (it floats beside the hand in 29, and in 23-25 it is not our hand), a weapon rigid in the rotation of the hand (our fitted hand turns 60-150 deg in the run), a per-frame horse search (jumps).
- **Mounted attack 2H (29):** the weapon is the extension of the arm: its direction is the line of the forearm (elbow -> wrist), the shaft through the centre of the fist, 35 cm from the butt (`ARM_ACTIONS`, `ARM_GRIP`); nothing cuts it but the hand: no legs, torso, arms, head, and the horse does not hide it (`uo_action_hide` with `occluders` hand, `horse` 0).
- **Hands cut the shaft:** where the hands hold it (fall to the front = action 22, and 23-29) the shaft is removed on the hand's pixels within 3 px of the grip (`uo_grip_cut`, `GRIP_CUT_ACTIONS`), whichever is nearer to the camera in 3D.
- **Falls (21, 22):** the fist at 3/4 of the shaft height; the shaft turns into the axis of the body (pelvis -> head, the ball towards the head) as the body tips over and lies along it (last frame 8 / 2 deg from the body axis); lifted when it would dig into the floor.
- **Cuts:** the hands hide the shaft where they are in front of it; from directions 3 and 4 the back, neck and head hide it (`behind_torso`, `uo_torso_margin` 0.04); the original body outline cuts it to the pixel (`EXACT_BODY`).
- **Look:** metal and leather colours of the texture (`BRIGHTNESS` 2.1); outline 0.7 of the colour next to the rim, mixed 60% from the inside (`uo_outline_blend`): a darker shade of the surrounding colours, not black.
- QA: `python pipeline/test_twohand.py` (synthetic mace, 8 s).

- **Mounted 23-29, grip on the original hand (2026-10):** `uo_fist_sprite` moves the weapon per frame and direction onto the hand shown on the ORIGINAL sprite (it was 3-4 px off our fitted fist, so the shaft passed beside the hand); where the hand merges with the body the shift comes from the other directions or from `uo_fist_manual` (read by eye: 23-27 dir 1, 29 dir 1 frames 3-4). The original hand's pixels cut the shaft (`uo_grip_cut_orig`), so it reads as held. Mounted actions 23-28: the horse and the legs no longer hide the shaft (`uo_action_hide`, horse 0, legs_under 1); the torso still does.

- **Version 2 `Mace2H_staff.vd` (recipe `mace2h_staff.json`):** the weapon lies like the ORIGINAL black staff (anim 617 of the client) in EVERY action: per frame and direction it is turned about the grip onto the staff's line, the ball at the part of the staff that is longer counted from the hand (`uo_staff_actions` = all, `uo_staff_lines` carries 1049 lines, `uo_staff_plane` = all: the weapon is swung into the image plane onto the staff's direction, so a weapon pointing at the camera in the walk, run, stand and fidgets is not left a foreshortened stub). The grip positions along the shaft are the same as in version 1 (`Mace2H.vd`); 17 is held at the centre of the staff; the hands cut the shaft only in the actions of version 1 (`uo_grip_cut_only`). Frames without a visible staff (29 dir 4 frame 1) keep the pose of version 1.

- **Paperdoll, second pass:** rendered 6 x supersampled (was 3), the spikes 30% shorter on the gump and art (`SPIKE_SCALE` 0.7: at 90 px/m the animation's long spikes were too big), the metal's contrast raised (`METAL_CONTRAST` 1.9 over its local 5 x 5 mean, `METAL_LEVELS` 22 / 100 / 205) so that every spike has a lit and a dark side; `gump_*_hd.png` = the weapon alone rendered at 4 x the gump's resolution (1040 x 948, not quantized to the client's palette, for reference or a larger gump).
- **Paperdoll, third pass = from the ORIGINAL model (`pipeline/uo_render_mace2h_gump_orig.py MODEL.glb OUT`):** the pictures in `paperdoll/` are now rendered from the Meshy .glb itself (99k vertices, its own PBR textures, Cycles 48 samples, sun from the upper left, 4 x supersampled) instead of the animation's item; same layout/files as before (art 45 x 42). The original's spikes are tiny and thin at 90 px/m, so each of the 12 tip clusters is enlarged about its own axis (`SPIKE_LEN` 0.9, `SPIKE_WIDTH` 1.55: 30% smaller than the first try), exposure 0.2, contrast 1.3. The outline (and the art's outer ring) is the mean colour of the neighbouring pixels x 0.75 / 0.45, never true black. The 3 stubs of the collar where the ball meets the haft are not widened (they were a dark blob over the haft); the weapon is turned 25 deg about its axis (`ROLL`) so that no spike points at the camera and down onto the collar. The previous item-based renders come from `uo_render_mace2h_gump.py`.
