# Ribbon medal (neck, hangs between the breasts, static on the body)

**Current version: `Medal_all_actions.vd`** (all 35 actions x 5 directions) made by `pipeline/medal_sprite.py`: the necklace is DRAWN in 2D, not rendered. At 36 px/m a cord is 1-2 px wide, and a Cycles render of a 3D cord was a smudge whose width and pixel count jumped from frame to frame (the old recipe `medal.json` and the history below are that renderer). `medal_sprite.py` takes the cord axis and the medal (`necklace_paths.json`, from `make_medal_necklace.py --paths`), poses them like an item (skin weights of the skin under each point; the V on the chest and the medal rigid on `chest`), projects them with the UO camera and draws: the cord (the projected 3D path is smoothed along itself, Gaussian `--smooth` 8 points = 2.8 cm, before drawing: the snapped 3D path is not smooth and gave ragged, uneven edges; the 2nd pixel of the collar sits on the up / left side across the minor axis of the line): the V on the chest (rest height under `--thin-below` 1.50 m) a 1 px stair-step line (2 px strands merged into one red mass), the collar 2 px; two tones that differ little (136,26,32 / 112,18,24), chosen from the mask alone (light where the pixel above or to the left is free, dark where both are cord, specks under 3 px dropped), so the tone does not flip between frames; the two strands of the V are spread by `--vwiden` 1.4 cm per side so that they do not merge into one mass. Earlier: ONE flat colour (radius-1.0 stamps: always 2 pixel centres across; the two-tone cord flipped pixels between light and dark from frame to frame), drawn round the neck (`--collar 0.72`: the points that lie on the shoulders are pulled in towards the neck axis, z at most 1.55), the medal as a 6 px disc that faces the viewer (narrower from the side, not drawn from behind), connected to the V. A pixel is drawn only where the item is not behind the 3D body (depth) and inside the silhouette of the ORIGINAL body frame (+1 px). `--fixes fixes.json` ({"action,frame,dir": [dx, dy]}) moves single frames by whole pixels for hand corrections. All 35 actions take 30 s.
Measured on the red pixels per frame (walk / run, all directions): the frame-to-frame change 0.04-0.09 (the Cycles version: 0.13-0.25); the silhouette of the posed 3D body fits the original in the neck circle (`uo_align`: 0 px shift for 127 of 135 frames).
```
python pipeline/make_medal_necklace.py MEDAL.glb medal_body.glb --paths model/items/medal/necklace_paths.json    # the geometry (cord axis, medal)
python pipeline/medal_sprite.py model/items/medal/necklace_paths.json OUT --vd Medal_all_actions.vd                 # the sprites
python pipeline/item_gif.py ROOT out.gif --actions 02_run_unarmed --dirs 1 --crop auto --scale 6 --body-color 169,134,102   # ROOT/clothing -> OUT
```

## Final form of the cord (iterations 2026-11)
A simple 1 px line, the same in every action and direction (all 1050 frames have it): the smoothed projected path is reduced to straight pieces (Ramer-Douglas-Peucker, `--rdp` 0.8 px for the V, `--rdp-collar` 1.8 px for the collar: straight lines have the fewest and most even steps), and it is drawn whole between the visible points. Which points are seen is decided per POINT: the V by the depth buffer, the collar by the side of the neck it is on (`--collar-facing`; the depth test cut the collar into dashes of changing length, and from behind the whole collar was missing in 112 of 1050 frames), runs shorter than 3 px are dropped. Collar pulled in to 0.82 of its width, V spread 1.4 cm per side, medal a 5.8 px disc facing the viewer. Hand corrections of single frames: `--fixes`.

Later: from `--thick-above` 1.455 m up (upper V and collar) the cord is 2 px, the second pixel on the OUTER side (the V: away from its middle, and only in rows where its two strands are 4+ px apart, so that they do not fuse in a profile; the collar: away from the neck); towards the medal 1 px. Cord pixels on or behind the head (own head depth buffer, `--head-tol`) are not drawn. The collar and the upper V are weighted to chest / neck / spine only (a raised arm in the attacks dragged them up over the face). Known: in a few attack frames with the torso turned to the side (09_attack_1h_slash dir 0, frames 0, 1, 5, 6) the two strands and the collar cross in the picture like a plus.

## History: the Cycles-rendered version
(`Medal_walk_run_attack1h.vd`, removed: actions 00 walk, 02 run, 09 attack 1H slash (5 directions); other actions: `--vd` without `--actions` (all 35, minutes).

Source: a Meshy-made glTF (`ribbon medal`, the user's file, **not in the repo**; check the licence). A flat loop of ribbon of 1.9 m does not fit a body, so `pipeline/make_medal_necklace.py` rebuilds it in body coordinates:
- the medal (disc, rim, recessed face and ring) is built procedurally, 17 cm and draped on the skin of the sternum (thickness kept, 3 mm off the skin, skin line smoothed over the mesh),
- the ribbon is a closed 6 cm strip (3.2 cm was 1 px at 36 px/m: a broken line in the sprites; recipe scene `uo_hide_erode` 2, `uo_outline` 1.0 = no dark rim: 0 is the darkest, 1 leaves the rim as it is) (3 mm thick, 12 mm off the skin: the posed skin of the neck and shoulders poked through a ribbon lying on it, `--ribbon-gap`) from the ring in a V up the chest, over the collar bones and the sides of the neck, round the nape; every point snapped to the skin (the body without the head, rest pose); the ribbon colour is painted into the texture (`--ribbon-rgb`, default dark red).

Rebuild (the .glb is made in a scratch folder):
```
python pipeline/make_medal_necklace.py MEDAL.glb medal_body.glb      # options: --medal-d, --ribbon-w, --ring-z, --gap, --ribbon-rgb
python pipeline/uo_make_item.py model/items/medal/medal.json --out OUT --vd --actions 00_walk_unarmed,02_run_unarmed,09_attack_1h_slash
```
The recipe (kind `vest`, `AUTOFIT` / `FIT` / `DENSIFY` off, `PART` all, `props` uo_behind_torso + uo_no_body_gap: the torso hides the necklace where it is behind it, e.g. the medal seen from the back): skin weights of the skin under each vertex, so the necklace follows the skin of the chest, shoulders and neck.

Weights: `"tune": {"uo_bind_item.py": {"MAP": "nearest"}}` (the default `under` took the head's weights for the ribbon at the base of the neck: 53 mm into the skin in the attack). Measured with ribbon 12 mm off the skin: inside the skin 0.3 %, deepest 14 mm, median 12.0 mm in every action (range 11.8-12.1). Earlier version (ribbon 3 mm off, `under`): (`pipeline/item_skin_gap.py`, posed body, walk / run / attack / spells / die): whole necklace median 3.7 mm off the skin (range 3.5-5.3 over all frames); rigid on the `chest` bone it had gone 57 mm into the skin in the attack. The medal alone: back 5 mm off the skin, constant over all actions (range 0.5 mm). Left: 3 % of the ribbon vertices inside the skin of the neck-shoulder valley (deepest 28 mm in the attack; the render keeps the necklace out of arms, hands and head, not out of the torso).
At 36 px/m the ribbon is about 1 px wide and the grey medal 3 px: it reads on the chest in the front view only.

## Medal cut in half (fixed)
The medal skinned like the skin under it folded up when the spine bent (its lower half is on spine / pelvis weights, the upper on the chest): in the walk the lower half of the disc was squashed into a flat sliver, so the sprite showed half a disc. Now the medal and the lower V (`"rigid_zone": {"below": 1.41, "xmax": 0.08, "bone": "chest"}` in `medal.json`: 1952 vertices 100 % on `chest`) are rigid; the rest of the ribbon follows the skin. The medal is built procedurally in `make_medal_necklace.py` (disc, rim, recessed face, ring; flat colour patches painted into flat spots of the normal map) because the file's own medal had red patches of the ribbon atlas on its faces and smooth shading that made it a lump. Medal 17 cm, face brighter than the rim, `METAL` off (no specular: the rim stays darker than the face). Measured: lower medal 17-24 mm off the skin in every action, inside the skin 0-1 %.

## Round cord, alignment (work in progress)
The ribbon is modelled as a round cord of 4.5 cm diameter (`--ribbon-w`): a flat strip changed its projected width with the angle it was seen at (edge-on on the shoulders it was a broken 1-px line); the cord has the same width from every side (flicker of the red pixel count between frames 0.24 -> 0.13). The path lies lower on the shoulders (z 1.49-1.51) so that it does not cover the face. `render_uo_layer.py` got `uo_align` (scene property, px): per frame the model body is moved by whole pixels onto the original body in a circle round the neck (shifts.json next to the frames); with the model's silhouette this was 0 px for 127 of 135 frames of walk / run / attack.
`item_gif.py --body-color R,G,B` tints the grey original body (a GIF with the skin colour of the game).
