Files in this folder:
- `Lute_poses.blend` (bpy 4.2): the body, the rig and every pose as an object `Lute_<item>` (the table above; `Lute_carry` is in the collection Clothing, the others in the collection `Lute_poses`, which is not rendered). Custom properties `uo_actions` and `uo_render_flags` say for which actions and with which extra render settings. `python pipeline/lute_select_pose.py Lute_poses.blend Lute_stand OUT.blend` moves one pose into Clothing.
- `render_lute.sh OUT_DIR`: the whole render, pose by pose (about 15 minutes), into one `OUT_DIR/clothing.vd`.
- `frames/`: the rendered PNG frames and `meta.json` of `Lute_layer.vd` (vdtool layout; `python vdtool/vdtool.py pack frames out.vd` packs them again).
- `Lute_layer.vd`: the result. The source model is `models/lute.glb` (Meshy, from the user); `lute_*.json`: the item recipes.

Gumps and art (`gumps/`, `python pipeline/uo_render_lute_gump.py ITEM.blend gumps`, item.blend of any lute recipe): `gump_{male,female}_1h` = the lute upright in the character's right fist like the client's weapons (the neck in the fist, headstock up, 12 deg out), `gump_{male,female}_2h` = held across the belly with both hands (its axis along the line from fist to fist of the body gumps 12 / 13, headstock at the character's right hand; the fists are 0.97 m apart, so they hold the lute near its two ends), 260 x 237, 90.5 px/m, the fists cut over the lute; `art` / `art_mirror` = the lute lying on the ground, strings up, seen from above. `*.png` + `*.bin` (a Gumpart.mul / art.mul entry), `preview.png` = the gumps on the body gumps and the art. The mul ids (50000 + AnimID male / 60000 + AnimID female, the art's item id) are for the user to assign.

2h options (`gumps/2h_options/`, `preview_2h_options.png`): `uo_render_lute_gump.py ... --set HEADSTOCK_LEFT=False --set LUTE_2H=1.5 --set Z_NECK=0.80 [--set ANG_2H=-8] [--set CUT=False]`: the lute scaled x1.35 / x1.5 so that one fist holds the neck near the headstock and the other the bowl (the fists of the body gump are 0.97 m apart); `bowl_left_*` = bowl at the character's right hand like the stand animation, `headstock_left_1.5` = the other way round; `_whole` = nothing cut out under the fists.
