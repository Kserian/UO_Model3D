---
name: uo-items-render
description: Item pipeline from free 3D models (uo_make_item.py, JSON recipes, autofit, conform, robes), texture quality, UO_Look lighting, materials, mesh density and weights. Use when adding/fixing an item or changing render, lighting or materials.
---

- One command: `python pipeline/uo_make_item.py recipe.json --preview` (recipe: `file`, `kind` or `weapon`, `skip`/`keep`; `--list` shows the meshes). Chain: `uo_import_item.py` (`PLACE = wrap`) → `uo_materials.py` → `uo_prepare_item.py` (`uo_autofit_item.py`, densify, fit with `LIMIT`, bind) → `render_uo_layer.py`. Weapon: `uo_orient_weapon.py` → `uo_place_weapon.py` → `uo_bind_item.py`. Measurements: `test_autofit.py`. `PLACE = height` only for items similar to the reference.
- Instrument / 1H item for either hand (horn): recipe `"weapon": {"class": "mace", "part": "weapon1h" | "weapon.L", "length": 0.6, "tip": "+x"}` (`tip` = model axis of the end that points up; `auto` fails on a thin shell). Right = bone `weapon1h.R`; left = `weapon.L` (grip of `weapon1h.R` mirrored onto `hand.L`, rigid on the hand, no original to calibrate on). Two recipes = two `.vd`.
- Robe from a cloth simulator (solid made of panels): `"outer_shell": 0.006` + `"conform"` + `"prepare": {"HEM": 0.15}`; what the user sees is measured by `item_body_holes.py`.
- Shirts/tunics from another mannequin: `"conform"` (`uo_conform_item.py`: sleeves onto arm axes, wrap 1.5 cm off the skin, render keeps 8 mm off the skin); check with preview `all`, measure with `item_clearance.py`. `"conform": {"SLEEVE_END": 0.02}` shortens or lengthens the sleeves to end 2 cm past the wrist (gambeson: they ended 9 cm short; arm skin in the 3D check fell ~3x).
- Mesh/weights: densify low-poly (`uo_densify_item.py`), weights like the skin underneath (SMOOTH 4, STIFF 1).
- Texture quality: 512 samples (adaptive, threshold 0.002, min 64), no `DESPECKLE`, rim `OUTLINE_TARGET` (edge/one pixel deeper = 0.38; an own `uo_outline` wins), Standard tone mapping. `test_items` 0.723, `test_canvas` 16/16.
- Light: keep `UO_Look` (Lambert, L = (0.0012, −0.7572, 0.6532), ambient 0.0798). Specular only on metal (`uo_materials.py`). No AO, no ground shadow.
- Per item (recipe `"scene"`): `uo_samples` (e.g. 1024, default 512) and `uo_edge_floor` (e.g. 24: the 1-px outline is never darker than 24/255 per channel, so a black item has no pure black rim); both off by default, baselines unchanged. Part option `"widen": {"width": 0.07}` makes straps (ribbons) wider in their own plane (a 3 cm strap is 1 px). Part option `"skin_wrap": {"GAP": 0.008}` lays straps on the skin (touching, thickness kept, weights of the skin under each vertex): the fit alone leaves a harness 3 cm off the skin; the renderer pushes items out of arms, hands, legs and head (`BODY_GAP`), not out of the torso.
- Contrast curve per item (recipe `"scene": {"uo_contrast": 1.6}`, `uo_contrast_pivot` 0.49): `rgb = pivot + k (rgb - pivot)` on the item's own pixels after the outline; the UO robes are painted with dark creases (sleeve brightness spread 54, our smooth shell 31: sleeves and hands read thicker). k 1.5 = 45, 1.8 = 52; default off (renders unchanged: 85/85 frames identical). Not a light change. Tried without effect on sleeves: `SHADOW` 0.4 -> 0.1, normal strength, `GREY_CLOTH` contrast, `OCCLUSION`; light direction left/right/above only 34-36; `uo_inner_line` works (40) but draws a line round the arm: the user rejected it. Table in README section 4.
- Contact shadows (per item, scene `uo_shade_sleeve`, `uo_shade_body`, `uo_shade_width` px): smooth gradients (not a line) where the sleeve meets the torso part (armpit, side, inside of the sleeve) and next to the body parts that hide the item, computed per frame from the label render (`uo_region`) and the hidden-body mask. Off by default (85/85 frames identical). The static `OCCLUSION` of the conform step did nothing for arms: it is baked in the rest pose, where the arms are away from the torso. 0.6 is slightly too dark; try 0.4-0.5, width 3-4, with `uo_contrast` 1.4-1.5. Table in README section 4.
- Base look of a robe / skirt (decided 2026-10-20, README section 4): `uo_make_item.py` `scene_of()` gives recipes of kind `robe` / `skirt` `uo_shade_sleeve` 0.4, `uo_shade_width` 4, `uo_contrast` 1.5 (the recipe's own `"scene"` wins). Silk robe, walk, sleeve zone: brightness spread 31 -> 46 (469: 54), dark share around the hands 38% -> 55% (469: 56%). Open: the mean brightness of the sleeve is 97 (469: 113) and the lights 155-163 (469: 180): try `uo_contrast_pivot` 0.55 and a lighter cloth; the gold trim gets more saturated.
- Harp (hand instrument held by a thin pillar, `model/items/harp/`, `uo_harp.py`): model = the user's Celtic harp glTF with the 20 strings cut out by `harp_prepare_model.py` (matte, `METAL: false`; the thin arm of the V is the held pillar). Recipe `"harp": {"HEIGHT": 0.68, "WIDEN": 1.25, "THICKEN": 0.019, "GRIP": 0.5, ...}`; the harp rides on a new root bone `harp` (keyed in all 35 actions; the arms are the original animation's, only the harp moves). User decisions per action (lists are settings of `uo_harp.py`): one hand = from BEHIND by the pillar at half height, pillar upright, the rest forward, orientation = the chest's (a sword's swing made it sway), walk/run (`STEADY`) one constant orientation per action; walk + walk armed (`LEAN_WALK`) = held NORMALLY by the pillar in the fist (NOT a flat tray crossing the hand: the user rejected that reading of "rownolegle do podloza"), leaned 12 deg outward away from the legs and tipped 20 deg to the ground; area spell + mounted 1H attack (`YAW_OUT`) = "wychylona na zewnatrz": the usual hold turned a FEW degrees (12) about the axis of the held side, the pillar (NOT the pillar along the fist's axis: the user rejected that reading); both deaths (`FLAT_DIE`) = in the hand, lying flat, sound box away from the body; both hands (`TWO_HAND` = stand, fidget 1+2, mounted stand, salute, eat; NOT combat idles / bow): left fist 0-5 cm from the wood, triangle facing the front (`FRONT_PLANE`); stand + mounted stand (`PARALLEL`) = parallel to the belly, pillar upright (no tilt), no collisions (2.5 cm clear), ahead of the chest (`FRONT`), mounted stand like stand; salute/eat pillar near the right fist's axis (`FIST_FREE`; a looser limit elsewhere: a strict one turned the triangle edge-on in stand). Size: the hands are 0.5-0.6 m apart in stand, so a torso-sized harp has to be wide (0.55 m) to reach both (0.56 m high with the original proportions did not, 0.66-0.68 high and 1.25x wide does); `THICKEN` because a 2 cm frame is <1 px. `uo_harp.py` logs per action the numbers each of these is checked with (plane from horizontal, pillar from vertical, touch distance, fist angle, ahead of the chest): read them after every change, then look at renders.
