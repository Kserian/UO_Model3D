---
name: uo-items-render
description: Item pipeline from free 3D models (uo_make_item.py, JSON recipes, autofit, conform, robes), texture quality, UO_Look lighting, materials, mesh density and weights. Use when adding/fixing an item or changing render, lighting or materials.
---

- One command: `python pipeline/uo_make_item.py recipe.json --preview` (recipe: `file`, `kind` or `weapon`, `skip`/`keep`; `--list` shows the meshes). Chain: `uo_import_item.py` (`PLACE = wrap`) → `uo_materials.py` → `uo_prepare_item.py` (`uo_autofit_item.py`, densify, fit with `LIMIT`, bind) → `render_uo_layer.py`. Weapon: `uo_orient_weapon.py` → `uo_place_weapon.py` → `uo_bind_item.py`. Measurements: `docs/qa/autofit.md`. `PLACE = height` only for items similar to the reference.
- Robe from a cloth simulator (solid made of panels): `"outer_shell": 0.006` + `"conform"` + `"prepare": {"HEM": 0.15}` (`docs/qa/robe_black.md`); what the user sees is measured by `item_body_holes.py`.
- Shirts/tunics from another mannequin: `"conform"` (`uo_conform_item.py`: sleeves onto arm axes, wrap 1.5 cm off the skin, render keeps 8 mm off the skin); check with preview `all`, measure with `item_clearance.py`, `docs/qa/jedi_tunic.md`. `"conform": {"SLEEVE_END": 0.02}` shortens or lengthens the sleeves to end 2 cm past the wrist (gambeson: they ended 9 cm short; arm skin in the 3D check fell ~3x).
- Thin weapon (bow limb, shaft) renders as a 1-px line: `"weapon": {"inflate": 0.012, "inflate_thin": 0.003}` moves vertices along normals (thin strands such as a bow string less; 0.02 everywhere made the string thick and the grip cover the fist) with scene `uo_outline` 0.6 for a softer rim; `"butt"` (m along the hand line) sets the height; originals hold a bow by its middle (`butt` = -length/2; bow.L originals span -0.44..+0.42 m along the line), weapon files are not committed. Bow shooting action 18: `uo_bow_hold.py` (default for class bow, `"hold": false` = off) re-keys bow.L in every action: handle (belly-most part of the middle: the bounds axis of a curved bow lies ~6 cm towards the string) in the centre of the fingers, string side towards the back of the pelvis (the chest is turned against the legs in the war stance 7) (bow in the body plane, edge-on from the front; held out in 18/19: along the arm towards the shoulder), shaft upright like in the walk (follows the body, pelvis to neck, only when it leans more than `FREE_LEAN` 35 deg; `PARALLEL`); in 18/19 the string comes along the mean shoulder line of the action (constant roll, the torso twist is not followed); in the ready stances 7 and 8 the shaft starts from the direction calibrated on the original bows and is tilted (<= `CLEAR_CONE` 90 deg) until the bow keeps `CLEAR_GAP` 2.5 cm off the posed skin and stays `IN_FRONT` 10 cm ahead of the chest centre (BVH test of the bow vertices; a bow under the armpit is rejected); in a fall the string side faces the chest and the bow lies along the body (smoothstep 35-60 deg of lean); rigid bow gets `uo_no_body_gap` (the skin push made a blob at its lower end) (calibrated keys jump ~87 deg between frames, pivot up to 10 cm off the fist). Not checked against original bow sprites (client not reachable from the session).
- Thin items (bow string, thin shafts) drop out between pixel centres: scene `"uo_filter": 1.0` (pixel filter 1 px = area average) + `"uo_alpha": 0.35` (coverage that counts as item) keep them continuous and thin, `"uo_samples": 1024` for more samples (render_uo_layer.py `FILTER_WIDTH`, `ALPHA_MIN`).
- Die backward (22): `AWAY_FROM_HEAD` turns the lying bow 10 deg so that its end nearest to the head moves away from it.
- Bow string drawn by the second hand: `uo_make_item.py` marks thin vertices (`uo_string`), `uo_bow_hold.py` keeps the straight line on the string side (`STRING_BAND`) and weights it to a bone `string.pull` (child of bow.L) that follows the fingers of hand.R in action 18 when the hand is at the face (`DRAW_FAR` / `DRAW_NEAR`); the limbs do not flex. Falls (21, 22): `AWAY_FROM_HEAD`.
- Bow in the shooting action 18 follows the ORIGINAL bow sprites: recipe `"orig": {"vd": ".../anim_0649.vd", "length": 0.86}` (anim.mul 649 via `vdtool/mul2vd.py`, client from the Drive link in uo-client-data; the Google Drive host must be allowed in the environment network settings; unrar from rarlab.com tar). `uo_make_item.py` exports the hand poses (`weapon_pose_export.py`), `uo_bow_hold.py` fits shaft direction and roll of the real bow mesh (scaled to the original's 0.86 m) to the 5 directions of every frame by chamfer to the sprite masks (the calibrated stick alone was off in the draw frames 3-4: 53 / 85 deg tilt instead of ~25). Re-fit of the stick calibration on the current body gives the same 1.7 px as before (`weapon_fit.py class`), so `weapon_motion.json` did not change. The string is only the thread (`STRING_BAND`, `STRING_WIDTH`, plus every thin vertex within 8 mm of it: a split ring made a dark fan), limbs flex by `FLEX`. The shaft over the frames of an action is chosen by dynamic programming (candidates every 10 deg, sprite chamfer + `LAM_T` per degree of turn between frames + weak tilt prior), then refined; mounted actions 23-29 use the calibrated shaft, 27 (mounted bow) is fitted like 18. Roll stays at the string-to-archer side (a free roll twisted in the hand). The string is ~0.8 px thick: recipe `inflate_thin` 0.0055, scene `uo_alpha` 0.41 (a continuous 1-px line, never 2 px; <0.5 px dropped out in pieces).
- Bow per-action rules (`uo_bow_hold.py`): `CONE_BY` = clearance search (shaft tilted up to N deg until the bow keeps `CLEAR_GAP` off the posed skin; BVH on the body), `SHIFT_BY` = the bow may also move sideways (mounted 23-25 hang vertical, shifted 9-18 cm away from the rider's thigh), `IN_FRONT_ACTIONS`, `ROLL_ONLY` = one constant roll per action chosen by the original sprites (15, 17, 27). The bow gets `uo_behind_torso` (the torso hides the part behind it, scene `uo_torso_margin` 0.08) so a bow in front of the belly is not drawn over the back.
- Fixing frames of a finished .vd: render only the affected actions (`run_render_headless.py ... ONLY=[...]`, `LAYER="clothing"`) and put them in with `pipeline/vd_patch.py OLD.vd NEW.vd OUT.vd action:dir:frame ...` (`*` = all, 0-based; palettes of the touched blocks are rebuilt from the old one, untouched blocks stay byte for byte). Bow variants used: string 1 px (`inflate_thin` 0.0085, `uo_alpha` 0.4) for action 18, `uo_holdout_margin` 0.001 where the grip covered the hand (fidget 2). Combat advance = viewer "Walk (war)" (15) is slanted 12 deg with the string to the body (`WALK_LEAN`); the viewer names: Walk (war) = 15, not walk_armed (1), 16 frame 3 is the mean of 2 and 4 (`SMOOTH_FRAMES`), 17 frame 3 clears the body (`CONE_FRAMES`), 23 / 24 / 26 are fitted to the original bow like 18.

- Mesh/weights: densify low-poly (`uo_densify_item.py`), weights like the skin underneath (SMOOTH 4, STIFF 1).
- Texture quality: 512 samples (adaptive, threshold 0.002, min 64), no `DESPECKLE`, rim `OUTLINE_TARGET` (edge/one pixel deeper = 0.38; an own `uo_outline` wins), Standard tone mapping. `test_items` 0.723, `test_canvas` 16/16 (`docs/qa/render_quality.md`).
- Light: keep `UO_Look` (Lambert, L = (0.0012, −0.7572, 0.6532), ambient 0.0798). Specular only on metal (`uo_materials.py`). No AO, no ground shadow.
- Mounted dir 1 of 23 / 24 (the bow lay over the torso and leg, the hand was not cut): the shaft of 23 / 24 is fitted to dir 1 only (`FIT_DIRS`), the handle follows our fist in both (`REL_SMOOTH`), and ONLY dir 1 of these two actions is patched into the full vd (`vd_patch.py ... 23:1:* 24:1:*`). 23 dir 1 also hides the bow where the original body is in front of it (bow pixels on the original body silhouette further than 3 px from the original bow sprite), 24 dir 1 does not need it. Do not change the 3D pose of a whole action for one direction: render it, patch the direction.
