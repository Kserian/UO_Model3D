---
name: uo-items-render
description: Item pipeline from free 3D models (uo_make_item.py, JSON recipes, autofit, conform, robes), texture quality, UO_Look lighting, materials, mesh density and weights. Use when adding/fixing an item or changing render, lighting or materials.
---

- One command: `python pipeline/uo_make_item.py recipe.json --preview` (recipe: `file`, `kind` or `weapon`, `skip`/`keep`; `--list` shows the meshes). Chain: `uo_import_item.py` (`PLACE = wrap`) → `uo_materials.py` → `uo_prepare_item.py` (`uo_autofit_item.py`, densify, fit with `LIMIT`, bind) → `render_uo_layer.py`. Weapon: `uo_orient_weapon.py` → `uo_place_weapon.py` → `uo_bind_item.py`. Measurements: `test_autofit.py`. `PLACE = height` only for items similar to the reference.
- Instrument / 1H item for either hand (horn): recipe `"weapon": {"class": "mace", "part": "weapon1h" | "weapon.L", "length": 0.6, "tip": "+x"}` (`tip` = model axis of the end that points up; `auto` fails on a thin shell). Right = bone `weapon1h.R`; left = `weapon.L` (grip of `weapon1h.R` mirrored onto `hand.L`, rigid on the hand, no original to calibrate on). Two recipes = two `.vd`.
- Robe from a cloth simulator (solid made of panels): `"outer_shell": 0.006` + `"conform"` + `"prepare": {"HEM": 0.15}`; what the user sees is measured by `item_body_holes.py`.
- Shirts/tunics from another mannequin: `"conform"` (`uo_conform_item.py`: sleeves onto arm axes, wrap 1.5 cm off the skin, render keeps 8 mm off the skin); check with preview `all`, measure with `item_clearance.py`. `"conform": {"SLEEVE_END": 0.02}` shortens or lengthens the sleeves to end 2 cm past the wrist (gambeson: they ended 9 cm short; arm skin in the 3D check fell ~3x).
- Mesh/weights: densify low-poly (`uo_densify_item.py`), weights like the skin underneath (SMOOTH 4, STIFF 1).
- Texture quality: 512 samples (adaptive, threshold 0.002, min 64), no `DESPECKLE`, rim `OUTLINE_TARGET` (edge/one pixel deeper = 0.38; an own `uo_outline` wins), Standard tone mapping. `test_items` 0.723, `test_canvas` 16/16.
- Light: keep `UO_Look` (Lambert, L = (0.0012, −0.7572, 0.6532), ambient 0.0798). Specular only on metal (`uo_materials.py`). No AO, no ground shadow.
- Per item (recipe `"scene"`): `uo_samples` (e.g. 1024, default 512) and `uo_edge_floor` (e.g. 24: the 1-px outline is never darker than 24/255 per channel, so a black item has no pure black rim); both off by default, baselines unchanged. Part option `"widen": {"width": 0.07}` makes straps (ribbons) wider in their own plane (a 3 cm strap is 1 px). Part option `"skin_wrap": {"GAP": 0.008}` lays straps on the skin (touching, thickness kept, weights of the skin under each vertex): the fit alone leaves a harness 3 cm off the skin; the renderer pushes items out of arms, hands, legs and head (`BODY_GAP`), not out of the torso.
- Contrast curve per item (recipe `"scene": {"uo_contrast": 1.6}`, `uo_contrast_pivot` 0.49): `rgb = pivot + k (rgb - pivot)` on the item's own pixels after the outline; the UO robes are painted with dark creases (sleeve brightness spread 54, our smooth shell 31: sleeves and hands read thicker). k 1.5 = 45, 1.8 = 52; default off (renders unchanged: 85/85 frames identical). Not a light change. Tried without effect on sleeves: `SHADOW` 0.4 -> 0.1, normal strength, `GREY_CLOTH` contrast, `OCCLUSION`; light direction left/right/above only 34-36; `uo_inner_line` works (40) but draws a line round the arm: the user rejected it. Table in README section 4.
- Contact shadows (per item, scene `uo_shade_sleeve`, `uo_shade_body`, `uo_shade_width` px): smooth gradients (not a line) where the sleeve meets the torso part (armpit, side, inside of the sleeve) and next to the body parts that hide the item, computed per frame from the label render (`uo_region`) and the hidden-body mask. Off by default (85/85 frames identical). The static `OCCLUSION` of the conform step did nothing for arms: it is baked in the rest pose, where the arms are away from the torso. 0.6 is slightly too dark; try 0.4-0.5, width 3-4, with `uo_contrast` 1.4-1.5. Table in README section 4.
- Base look of a robe / skirt (decided 2026-10-20, README section 4): `uo_make_item.py` `scene_of()` gives recipes of kind `robe` / `skirt` `uo_shade_sleeve` 0.4, `uo_shade_width` 4, `uo_contrast` 1.5 (the recipe's own `"scene"` wins). Silk robe, walk, sleeve zone: brightness spread 31 -> 46 (469: 54), dark share around the hands 38% -> 55% (469: 56%). Open: the mean brightness of the sleeve is 97 (469: 113) and the lights 155-163 (469: 180): try `uo_contrast_pivot` 0.55 and a lighter cloth; the gold trim gets more saturated.
- Cloak (kind `cloak`, decided 2026-10-22 with a Meshy cape, recipe `"stretch": [0.7, 0.7, 0.7]` for a model that is ~1.4x too big, `"turn": 0` + `TURN_BACK` false): `uo_make_item.py` runs `uo_cloak_hug.py` (upper cape 12 mm off the skin of the shoulders / back, moved only along y, slope-corrected gap, no vertex inside the body; an earlier push along the skin normal wrapped it over the chest, one along y only left 28 % of the upper vertices inside the body = grey stripes) and `uo_cloth_sim.py` with `CLOAK_SIM` (cotton, pin only at the shoulder band, hem goal 0.12, `swing` 0.5 = the original 468 swing as a weak target, gravity 1.5; hem goal 0.04 collapsed the hem to 0.3-0.5 m in walk / run, 0.12 keeps 0.8 m). Measure with the gap to the posed skin per action (upper cape median 1.5-3.5 cm, 0 inside). Blender's wind force field (`wind` key) was tried first and was not stable (cape bunched at the hips): off by default. The render reads the simulation as the cape's displacement (`cloth_push`, type cloak); without `cloth_sim.npz` it falls back to the `cloak_pitch.json` swing.
- Scene defaults of a cloak (`CLOAK_SCENE`): `uo_horse_holdout` 0 (the horse does not cut a cape; the client draws a cloak over the mount) and `uo_supersample` 3 (`render_uo_layer.py` `SUPERSAMPLE`: the item's colour from K x K sub-samples averaged over the pixel, alpha / silhouette of the pixel centre, so masks are identical; only the clothing passes, never the visible body; +20 % time; error vs a 7 x 7 reference 0.95 -> 0.36 levels). The material step keeps `FILTER_TEXELS_PER_M` 162 for a cloak (the default 54 reduced the baked 2048 px texture to 204 px).
- Baked texture (`uo_import_item.py`): `BAKE_FILL` takes the unbaked texels (black background and gaps inside islands; 2.4 % of the faces had a black texel at their centre) from the nearest baked texel; `BAKE_UV_ANGLE` / `BAKE_RAY` changed nothing. A Meshy cape has its gold trim only at the lower corners (about 9 px at 36 px/m) and two brooches (< 2 px): that is all a UO frame can show.
- Cloak motion, revised 2026-10-23 (user: the cloth solver looked ragged, jittery and slid off the shoulders; "look at the original"): the original cloak 468 is a smooth, even, slightly flared sheet pinned at the shoulders: stand and walk hang (walk: hem a few deg), run streams back almost flat (60-87 deg from vertical at the shoulders, drooping to the hem), riding leaves the shoulders backwards and curves down over the rump (stand: lies on it). `uo_cloak_motion.py` (default for a cloak, ~2 min for all 35 actions) reproduces that: `cloak_pitch.json` angles smoothed in time, `cloth_lib.cloak_bend` (centre line = integral of the turn), a small gait wave, kept behind the posed body along y only, riding: dropped onto the horse proxy (ray down, field smoothed 4x) and pushed out of it, all as smooth fields; displacement 0 on the shoulder line. `uo_cloak_hug.py` also raises the cape 3 cm (`RAISE`) and narrows the hem to 0.85 (`HEM_TAPER`); scene `uo_edge_smooth` 2 for a cloak. Check with the profile silhouette vs the sprite 468 (dir 2, centroid and extent per action). The cloth solver stays available with recipe `"sim": {"solver": "cloth"}`.
- Cloak geometry, revised 2026-10-23 (user: the Meshy cape was ragged, flared like a dress, its top lay off the back): the generated shell (30k decimated triangles, A-line flare, brooch tabs, fine pleats) can not look like the original cloak 468 (an even, straight rectangle), neither moved by the cloth solver (ragged hem, jitter, the free part collapsed to a strip in walk / run) nor kinematically. `uo_make_cape.py` (recipe `"cape": {}`, `"file"` only for the colours; AUTOFIT / DENSIFY / FIT off, no hug) builds a regular 2.5 cm silk sheet instead: 0.48 m wide at the shoulders to 0.66 m, from the shoulders (top edge follows them, `SHOULDER_COVER`) to 0.10 m above the ground, its rest shape the upper envelope of the skin of the back (rests on shoulder blades and buttocks, never inside), texture (u across, v along: no UV problems) from the recipe model's colours with a gold hem band and gentle folds (`FOLDS`). `uo_pin_drop` puts the pivot of the motion 5 cm under the highest point (the collar follows the body). Default motion = `uo_cloak_motion.py`, which on this mesh gives straight even edges in every action; the hybrid (cloak_motion.npz next to the item makes `uo_cloth_sim.py` follow it: silk `density` 0.35, `hem_goal` 0.3) added a wobbly hem back and is not the default. Compare with the profile / back views of the sprite 468 (vdtool extract of `pipeline/body13/mul/anim_0468.vd`).
