Changes made here (`prepare_harness.py`): all parts except two straps and the sword (handle, handle wrap, guard, scabbard) removed; the straps rescaled to the UO body,
fitted, skinned to the torso; the sword moved and bound rigidly to the `chest` bone; materials converted to the UO look; rendered to UO frames (`.vd`).

The original `.glb` (15 MB) is not in the repository: download it from the source above (Sketchfab login) and pass its path to `prepare_harness.py`.
